What I work on

A language model turns speech into a symbolic command. A deterministic validator checks that command against the twin's state, the procedure and the user's intent, and has exactly two outcomes: it runs, or it is refused with an explanation. The 2023 paper describes the framework; the 2026 one measures what happens when the validator is removed.

I shipped web and mobile software in React, Next.js, Laravel and Flutter, and the research usually ends up as something that runs.

In progress, in public: retrieval scored against a hand-labelled set. Public data only.
